How every price is made

The exact derivation, with the model's own constants. Nothing is priced by name: the same functions score every row, and a row's detail view repeats this arithmetic with its own figures filled in.

  1. Anchor A — the median multiple. Over the category's launched peers (token-bearing, TVL > 0): A = median(mcapi / TVLi). A category needs at least 3 peers; below that, the global peer set prices the row and the detail view says so.
  2. Anchor B — the size fit. Least squares over the same peers: log(mcap) = a + b·log(TVL), slope b clamped to [0.4, 1.6]. It needs at least 8 peers and speaks only inside the peers' own TVL range, widened ×4. B = the fitted market cap at this row's TVL, divided by that TVL.
  3. Base multiple. base = clamp( A × sqrt(clamp(B/A, 0.5, 2)), 0.03, 2.5 ): the fit may move the median by at most ×1.41 either way. Where there is no fit, base = clamp(A, 0.03, 2.5).
  4. Anchor agreement. spread = max(A, B) / min(A, B). When spread > 2, agreement = 1 / (1 + log10(spread)); otherwise agreement = 1. A price the two anchors cannot agree on is discounted for the disagreement itself.
  5. Growth. growth = max(0.55, 1 + 0.6 × clamp(change_7d, −50, 100) / 100).
  6. Maturity. maturity = 0.75 + 0.45 × (1 − exp(−age_years / 1.8)); a protocol with no recorded age counts as 1 year.
  7. Breadth. breadth = 1 + 0.03 × min(chains − 1, 8).
  8. Product rule. product = 0.005 when the record marks a wrapped, staked or bridged product of another protocol, else 1: a wrapped or bridged product's own launch is a relisting of a token that already trades, priced at 0.5% of its peer value.
  9. Implied value. implied = TVL × base × agreement × growth × maturity × breadth × product, rounded to whole dollars and floored at $1. The band beside each price is the same arithmetic with the base replaced by the peers' 25th- and 75th-percentile multiples.
  10. Signal, 0–100. 100 × (0.40 × age percentile + 0.30 × 7-day momentum percentile + 0.15 × chain-breadth percentile + 0.15 × TVL standing inside its category), every percentile taken against this board's own rows.
